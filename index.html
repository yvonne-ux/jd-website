Work Personality Index

WPI

Work Personality Index

Work Personality Index (WPI) is designed to measure personality traits that relate to work performance and work environment.

  • Useful in selection and development
  • Focus on normal adult population
  • Be able to answer these questions:
    • Which skills are the individual good at?
    • What does the individual enjoy doing at work?
    • How does the individual typically behave at work?

It helps you identify key motivators and behaviours — essential for hiring the right people, developing employees, and supporting great leaders.

Employee Reliability Inventory

ERI

Employee Reliability Inventory

Employee Reliability Inventory (ERI) is a test that measures if a candidate's future behaviour is likely to be affected by any of these 6 factors.

  • Employment Stability — Long-term commitment
  • Courtesy — Commitment to service with external and internal customers
  • Productivity — Contribution to results
  • Emotional Maturity — Responsibility, good judgment, teamwork and tolerance for frustration
  • Trustworthiness — Reliability, no thefts
  • Safe Behavior — Accident prevention

Sales Competence Coefficient

CCV

Sales Competence Coefficient

Evaluates sales-specific competencies to help organisations build and strengthen high-performing sales teams, scoring candidates across six core traits:

Achievement Motivation

Concern for high performance standards as well as work on challenging tasks.

Self-Confidence

Confidence and security in one's own abilities and skills to achieve the objectives.

Persuasiveness

Need to express verbally with the intention of influencing the behaviour and decisions of others.

Initiative

Acting proactively, getting ahead of the facts and taking risks to achieve the goals.

Energy

Ability to perform productive behaviours for long periods of time.

Ego Strength

Ability to maintain a positive attitude towards challenges despite difficulties or rejection.

Each CCV report is easy to interpret: a Global Index benchmarks the candidate's score against the evaluated population, and every one of the six competencies above is presented as its own percentile score. Every report also contains:

  • Interpretation of the report
  • Significant behavioural indicators
  • Follow-up questions
  • Guidelines for development
